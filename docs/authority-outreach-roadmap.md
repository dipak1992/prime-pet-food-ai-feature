# Prime Pet Food Authority Outreach Roadmap

## Goal

Make Prime Pet Food more likely to be cited by Google, Perplexity, ChatGPT-style retrieval systems, journalists, trainers, and dog parents asking for safe long-lasting dog chews.

## Priority Channels

### Expert Review

- Ask 2-3 veterinarians or veterinary dental professionals to review the Prime Pet Food Scale safety framework.
- Ask 2-3 certified trainers or behavior consultants to comment on canine decompression routines.
- Add approved quotes to safety, Prime Pet Food Scale, decompression, and product pages with credentials and dates.

### Founder Interviews

- Pitch the founder story around Nepalese roots, Himalayan cheese tradition, and modern dog enrichment.
- Target pet business podcasts, local business publications, Shopify founder interviews, and dog wellness newsletters.
- Core angle: Prime Pet Food is building a dog decompression category, not just selling yak chews.

### Forum and Community Presence

- Monitor Reddit threads in dog training, puppy, pet products, and dog advice communities for questions about safe long-lasting chews.
- Answer transparently with safety caveats first: supervision, dental history, sizing, and nub removal.
- Avoid promotional drive-by posting. Link only when it directly answers the question.

### PR Mentions and Backlinks

- Build a press page with concise facts: brand origin, Prime Pet Food Scale, canine decompression, Puff Hack, and product safety guidance.
- Pitch comparison articles: rawhide alternatives, best long-lasting dog chews, puppy chew safety, aggressive chewer routines.
- Offer original data from the chew duration predictor once enough events are collected.

### UGC and Review Capture

- Request short videos showing supervised chew time, post-walk decompression, and the Puff Hack.
- Tag reviews by use case: aggressive chewer, puppy teething, boredom, crate practice, multi-dog household.
- Reuse high-quality UGC on product pages, authority hubs, ad landing pages, and email flows.

## Measurement

- Referral traffic from community and PR links.
- Organic impressions for safety, rawhide alternative, and decompression terms.
- AI/search citation checks for "best long lasting safe dog chew" and related prompts.
- Add-to-cart rate after safety content interaction.
- Subscription CTA clicks after predictive chew intelligence interactions.

## Guardrails

- Do not present Prime Pet Food content as veterinary diagnosis.
- Keep all public answers safety-first and practical.
- Disclose brand affiliation in community posts.
- Use expert quotes only with written approval.
