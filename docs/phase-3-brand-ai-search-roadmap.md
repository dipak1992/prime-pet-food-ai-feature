# Phase 3 Brand and AI Search Roadmap

## Goal

Make Prime Pet Food read as a founder-led Himalayan yak chew brand with deep breed-specific authority, controlled marketplace presence, and a credible product expansion path.

## Founder Narrative

- Use `/pages/about-us` as the primary story page.
- Lead with Dipak Regmi, Nepal origin, engineering-led product standards, Himalayan farming families, and Texas packing.
- Keep the proof visual: founder image, Himalayan source image, human craft process, Texas packing and quality checks.
- Replace generic support language with founder-led support language across chat, schema, and trust modules.

## AI Search Content System

Build breed pages as specific answer assets, not broad SEO landing pages. Each guide should include:

- Breed entity: full breed name, common aliases, AKC group where relevant.
- Chewing risk profile: jaw shape, bite behavior, typical anxiety triggers, boredom risk, and recommended supervision.
- Size guidance: Prime chew size recommendation by weight band and chewing style.
- Safety caveats: dental history, puppy/senior notes, nub removal, puff hack.
- Internal links: product page, chew duration tool, rawhide alternative guide, founder story, and relevant adjacent breed pages.

Priority cluster:

- Labrador Retriever
- Golden Retriever
- German Shepherd
- French Bulldog
- Beagle
- Poodle
- Boxer
- Rottweiler
- Husky
- Dachshund

Avoid positioning everything under the generalized "Prime Pet Food Scale." Keep the scale as a support framework, but make the public content architecture breed-led and question-led.

## Marketplace Control

- Audit TikTok Shop and other third-party listings weekly.
- Capture screenshots, seller IDs, product titles, URLs, pricing, and brand misuse.
- Prioritize takedowns for unauthorized Prime Pet Food use, confusing product titles, counterfeit risk, and pricing dilution.
- Keep one internal log for seller, platform, violation type, evidence, date submitted, and resolution status.

## Product Innovation

Near-term product extensions should protect the simple-ingredient position:

- New shapes for chewing ergonomics and smaller dogs.
- Naturally flavored variants only if ingredient integrity stays clear.
- Functional bundles by routine: puppy starter, aggressive chewer, calm evening, multi-dog home.
- Subscription retention tests by size, chew frequency, and dog profile.

Do not chase a broad catalog too early. Expand around the core promise: Himalayan heritage, simple ingredients, longer supervised chew sessions, and clear safety education.
